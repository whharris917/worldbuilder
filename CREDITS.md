### Planets and stars

| Work | From | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720): colour and elevation | NASA Scientific Visualization Studio | The Moon (`planet_moon`) |
| [Blue Marble Next Generation](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry) and the GEBCO elevation image | NASA Earth Observatory | The Earth (`planet_earth`) |
| [Viking global colour mosaic](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_global_color_mosaic_925m) | USGS Astrogeology | Mars colour (`planet_mars`) |
| MOLA elevation (MEGDR) | NASA Mars Global Surveyor, PDS Geosciences | Mars relief (`planet_mars`) |
| Yale Bright Star Catalogue, as packaged at [brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog](https://github.com/brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog) | Yale University Observatory | The night sky (`game/data/stars.bin`) |

`tools/build_planets.py` and `tools/build_sky.py` name each file they use.
